Instructions

Every potion is a mole calculation. Read the recipe, work out the missing value, and drop your answer into the cauldron.

Core formula: moles = mass ÷ molar mass  ( n = m / M )

The Rules

  • 5 rounds per game, starting with 3 lives
  • Lose no lives in all 5 rounds to unlock a bonus 6th round
  • Answer before the timer runs out — faster answers earn bigger speed bonuses
  • Give every answer to exactly 3 significant figures
  • A wrong or late answer costs a life and shows the full working
  • Only relative atomic masses are given — you must build molar masses yourself!

Difficulty Levels

Easy — Convert between mass and moles of a single element. 30 seconds.

Medium — Convert between mass and moles of a compound (e.g. H₂O, CaCO₃). 60 seconds.

Hard — A balanced equation is given. From the amount of one substance, find the amount of another using the mole ratio. 100 seconds.

Expert — You are given the masses of both reactants. Determine the limiting reagent, then calculate the moles and mass of the product. 200 seconds.
