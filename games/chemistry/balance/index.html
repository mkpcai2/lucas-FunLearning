📖 Chemistry Revision Notes

1. Concentration

Add reactant or remove product → shifts Right (→) to consume added/make up for lost. Add product or remove reactant → shifts Left (←).

2. Temperature

Heating shifts in the endothermic direction (+ΔH) to absorb heat. Cooling shifts in the exothermic direction (-ΔH) to release heat.

3. Pressure

Increasing pressure shifts to the side with fewer gas moles. Decreasing pressure shifts to the side with more gas moles.
